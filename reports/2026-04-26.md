**Files modified** (from `git diff HEAD`):

- `SKILL-typescript.md` (+37 lines): Added **Known Issue #54** — *Linux CLI binary auto-discovery prefers musl variant over glibc, causing `ENOENT` / `EPIPE` on glibc systems*. Affects v0.2.116+ (when per-platform native binary packages were introduced). Three workarounds documented: (1) explicit `pathToClaudeCodeExecutable` pointing to the glibc binary, (2) npm `overrides` stub for musl packages, (3) Dockerfile symlink. Also notes legacy workaround (downgrade to ≤ v0.2.112) and pending PR #305.

- `SKILL-python.md` (+3 lines): Updated `SessionStore.append()` docstring to clarify retry semantics — failed batches are retried 3 times with short backoff before being dropped and surfaced as a `MirrorErrorMessage`; timeouts are *not* retried; recommended implementation is idempotent upsert. Replaces previous "at-most-once" description.
